About Triply

Planning a trip usually means juggling a map, a spreadsheet, and a dozen open tabs. Triply takes your preferences (transport, distance, cost, ratings, cuisine) and drafts a day-by-day itinerary with AI.

It is a Flutter prototype and has not been released.

Triply is an experiment. It has not been released and is not available to download.

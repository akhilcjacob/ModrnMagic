About Astro Defender

Astro Defender is the kind of game you open for two minutes and play for twenty. Earth sits in the middle of the screen and enemies close in. You rotate your ship and fire.

As you play you earn upgrades for your ship. The game plays fully offline, so it works on a plane or on the subway.

It is free to download on the App Store and Google Play and is supported by ads.
